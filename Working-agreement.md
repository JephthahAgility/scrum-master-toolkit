Team Working Agreement

Purpose

A Working Agreement establishes shared expectations for how the team communicates, collaborates, and works together.

Communication

- Communicate openly and respectfully.
- Raise blockers as early as possible.
- Ask questions when requirements or priorities are unclear.
- Keep relevant team members informed of important changes.

Scrum Events

- Attend Scrum events prepared and on time.
- Participate actively in discussions.
- Keep conversations focused on the purpose of each event.
- Respect everyone's opportunity to contribute.

Collaboration

- Support team members when help is needed.
- Share knowledge and information.
- Focus on solving problems rather than assigning blame.
- Encourage constructive feedback.

Accountability

- Team members take ownership of their commitments.
- Work should be kept visible and updated regularly.
- Impediments should be raised promptly.
- The team regularly reviews and improves its ways of working.

Continuous Improvement

The team will periodically review this Working Agreement during retrospectives and update it when improvements are identified.
